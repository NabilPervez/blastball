## Full description (≤4000)

Blastball is a sports league that plays itself — and it gets weirder every week.

Twelve teams of oddball players take the field whether you're watching or not. You're the fan in the stands: follow the games pitch by pitch, place bets with play coins, and spend your winnings on votes that rewrite the rules of the league itself.

WATCH EVERY GAME
• Live play-by-play with score, bases, outs and the latest action
• Speed games up 2× or 5×, or skip straight to the final
• The league keeps playing in the background — come back to see what you missed

BET WITH PLAY COINS
• Bet on any game with free in-game coins — no real money, ever
• Pick a favorite team and earn bonus coins when they win
• Climb the leaderboards and recover with bailouts when you go broke

VOTE TO BEND REALITY
• Every week the league holds an election
• Spend coins on votes for rule changes, strange weather and stranger blessings
• Watch your choices ripple through the seasons that follow

COLLECT THE PLAYERS
• Every player has a career, a life story and odd traits that spread to teammates
• Rarity-bordered player cards and a keepsake collection
• Honor fallen legends in the Hall of the Departed

YOUR LEAGUE, YOUR PACE
• Living mode: a new day every 15 minutes to once a day — your choice
• Manual mode: advance game by game, day by day or a week at a time
• Share a seed so friends can play the exact same universe
• Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking

Blastball is a simulation for fun. Coins have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn.
