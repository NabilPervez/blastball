Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Blastball (the website at blastball-sim.netlify.app and the Android app com.nabilpervez.blastball) does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your leagues, coins, bets, votes and settings are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Coins are play money with no cash value and cannot be bought or withdrawn.

Third-party services

The app is hosted on Netlify and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log standard technical data (such as IP address) to deliver files, under their own privacy policies.

Children

Blastball is not directed at children under 13.
